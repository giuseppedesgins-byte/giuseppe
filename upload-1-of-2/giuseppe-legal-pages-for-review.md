## 3. Seller Agreement (`/legal/seller-agreement`)

This Seller Agreement governs the relationship between Giuseppe and brands approved to
sell on the marketplace ("Sellers").

Giuseppe is intended to be operated by **The Giuseppe LLC**. **[Entity formation is not yet
confirmed as complete — do not state or imply Giuseppe is a formed legal entity, and do not
treat this as a binding agreement, until formation and state/country of incorporation are
confirmed.]**

- Giuseppe reviews and approves applications at its sole discretion; acceptance is not guaranteed.
- Sellers are solely responsible for fulfilling their own orders, including accurate shipping, packaging, and customer service.
- Sellers must represent products accurately and must not list counterfeit, replica, unauthorized, illegal, or materially misrepresented goods.
- New and updated products are submitted through the seller portal for Giuseppe's review and begin as Pending Review, not publicly visible. Only Giuseppe can approve a product to go live — Sellers cannot publish or approve their own listings.
- Sellers may mark a live product Out of Stock at any time without Giuseppe's approval — doing so immediately and completely removes it from all public listings (not shown as "Sold Out") and does not count against Seller performance. Returning an Out of Stock product to live requires the Seller to request reactivation and Giuseppe's approval.
- If a customer's paid order is cancelled because the Seller can't fulfill it, that is recorded as a Seller-caused cancellation and affects Seller performance standing — unlike marking an item Out of Stock before it sells, which carries no penalty.
- Giuseppe currently charges a commission of **9%** on the retained (non-refunded) portion of a sale; this rate may change with notice.
- Giuseppe does not retain its commission on refunded orders — a full refund means $0 commission retained; a partial refund recalculates commission on what's retained (e.g. $100 sale, $20 refunded, $80 retained → $7.20 commission).
- When a refund is issued for a reason within the Seller's control, the Seller is responsible for the payment processor's transaction fee on that sale (not refundable by the processor). This fee is not charged when the refund reason is outside the Seller's control.
- Funds from a sale are held as "pending" for **5 days** after the order is marked delivered before becoming available for payout. Giuseppe may also place a hold on a specific order, or on a Seller's funds generally, while an issue is being investigated.
- If a return, complaint, or case is opened on an order, Sellers are expected to respond within **3 days**; cases that go unanswered are automatically escalated to Giuseppe.
- Sellers may mark specific items "Final Sale" to opt out of change-of-mind returns; this never overrides a buyer's right to a return or refund for counterfeit, wrong, not-as-described, defective, damaged, missing, or never-arrived items.
- Giuseppe may request additional verification at any time and may suspend or remove a Seller for violating marketplace standards, including for confirmed counterfeit listings, which are removed immediately upon confirmation.
- Approved Sellers are expected to promote their Giuseppe launch on their own social channels for at least 24 hours.
- Payouts are made via Stripe Connect once the Seller completes Stripe's bank-account verification — Giuseppe does not hold or wire funds directly except for anything settled outside Giuseppe before the Seller connected an account.

**Taxes.** Sellers are solely responsible for determining, collecting (where applicable),

## 4. Marketplace Rules (`/legal/marketplace-rules`)

Sellers approved to sell on Giuseppe agree to the following standards:

- Products must be authentic, accurately described, and match submitted photography.
- No counterfeit, replica, unauthorized, illegal, or materially misrepresented products.
- Only Giuseppe can approve a new or updated listing to go live — submissions start as Pending Review and stay off the site until approved.
- Marking a product Out of Stock before it sells is responsible inventory management, not a violation — it requires no approval and carries no penalty. Bringing it back live does require Giuseppe's approval (reactivation).
- Orders must be processed and shipped within the timelines provided at application.
- Tracking must be provided where available and shared with Giuseppe/customers promptly.
- Customer inquiries must be answered promptly and professionally.
- Repeated violations may result in warnings, probation, suspension, or permanent removal.

**Enforcement.** Giuseppe may take enforcement action ranging from a warning to immediate,
permanent removal depending on severity. Serious violations — including counterfeit
merchandise, fraud, or repeated failure to ship — may result in immediate removal without
warning. A legitimate carrier loss, or an order cancelled because a Seller responsibly
flagged unavailable inventory before a sale, is not treated as Seller fault on its own.

## 5. Returns & Refunds (`/legal/returns`)

You can start a return from your order page within **14 days** of delivery, unless the item
is marked **Final Sale** on its product page. Final Sale items aren't eligible for
change-of-mind returns, but they're always eligible for a return or refund if the item is
counterfeit, the wrong item, not as described, defective, damaged in transit, missing a
piece, or never arrived.

Who pays for return shipping depends on why you're returning it: if the brand made a
mistake, the brand covers return shipping. If you're returning simply because you changed
your mind, you're responsible for return shipping unless the brand's own policy says
otherwise.

Start a return or report a problem from your order confirmation page. The seller has 3 days
to respond once you open a case; if they don't, it's automatically escalated to Giuseppe.

Refunds are issued back to your original payment method once a return is approved (or
immediately for no-return-required cases like a lost package).

*Note: this page pulls its numbers live from Giuseppe's admin settings, so it always
reflects the current return window, seller response deadline, and payout hold period
without needing a code change.*

## 6. Shipping (`/legal/shipping`)

Every brand on Giuseppe determines its own shipping destinations, methods, processing
time, and rates — shown at checkout before you pay. Orders are shipped directly by the
selling brand. If your order includes items from more than one brand, each is shipped and
tracked separately, since each brand checkout is independent on Giuseppe (Giuseppe does not
do unified multi-brand checkout).
